Institutional demand for hedges against AI risk is now explicit enough that NYMEX is listing futures on GPU rental costs. On prediction markets and perpetual futures, the same risk has been trading for months, and that data shows where the leverage in the AI trade actually sits, what the market expects compute to cost, and which legs of the trade are barely traded.
