A wallet-level cohort analysis of Polymarket across March to August 2026 shows liquidity is supplied by a few dozen addresses and that retail loses 14.5% on positions held to resolution. Only professional directional wallets, 271 of them taking 5.4% of aggressive flow, finish positive, while the World Cup drove all of the growth in sports.
